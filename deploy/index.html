Before you move

Good to know.

Who can apply to stay?

The residence welcomes female university and college students, working women and professionals, interns, trainees, and students coming to Islamabad from other cities.

What room types are available?

Executive cabins are available for one to four residents. Executive rooms are available for one to five residents and include attached washrooms. Furnished and non-furnished options are listed.

What is included?

Listed facilities include air conditioning, beds, mattresses, side and study tables, wardrobes with shoe racks, mirrors, UPS backup, car parking and high-speed internet. Three-time meals are included as specified for cabin accommodation.

What are the booking terms?

The published registration fee is Rs. 5,000, and a refundable two-month security deposit is compulsory. On-demand rooms require two security deposits in advance and a minimum one-year contract.
